Features

What you get

  • 01

    Logo

    Ten templates — wordmarks, lockups, monogram, badge, abstract mark. Live preview. Export SVG, PNG (512 · 1024 · 2048) and a favicon.

  • 02

    Palette

    Twenty hand-picked color combinations with mood labels and WCAG contrast badges, so you know which ones will actually read.

  • 03

    Typography

    Fifteen Google Fonts pairings — heading + body — chosen for readability and tone, from editorial serifs to developer mono.

  • 04

    Printable brand sheet

    One-page A4 guide with logo variants, palette swatches, type specimens and usage rules. Save to PDF in two clicks.

How it works

No backend. No database.

Templates, palettes and fonts are static JSON in this repo. The app reads them, you customize in the browser, and you download the result. Your kit never leaves your device unless you choose to save it as a GitHub Issue.
