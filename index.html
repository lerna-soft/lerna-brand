Features

What you get

  • 01

    Logo

    Pick a curated SVG template and customize wordmark, accent color, and icon. Export as SVG or PNG.

  • 02

    Palette

    Six hand-picked color combinations. Or build your own. Hex, HSL and contrast checks included.

  • 03

    Typography

    Curated Google Fonts pairings — heading + body — chosen for readability and tone.

  • 04

    One-page guide

    Generates a printable brand sheet so your designer or developer knows what to use where.

How it works

No backend. No database.

Templates, palettes and fonts are static JSON in this repo. The app reads them, you customize in the browser, and you download the result. Your kit never leaves your device unless you choose to save it as a GitHub Issue.
